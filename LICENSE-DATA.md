This covers:

- the downloads the site publishes (`data/`: hourly and daily counts, term lists, study files);
- the frozen study versions in `studies/`;
- the text of the site and of `docs/`.

You may copy, share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you credit the project. A suitable credit is: Emma Legrottaglie, Anti-trans rhetoric monitor, https://emmalegrottaglie.github.io/USMidterms-Transphobia/ (see `CITATION.cff`).

Two things are not ours to licence:

- The counts are aggregate hourly totals computed from Twitter's counts API. They contain no post text, usernames or account IDs. Twitter's own terms still apply to anything you fetch from Twitter yourself.
- The Context tab and `docs/CONTEXT-ADS.md` report figures and short quotations from other organisations (AP, AdImpact, the Wesleyan Media Project, CNN, The Advocate, FactCheck.org, Metro Weekly, the Human Rights Campaign, GLAAD and Morning Consult). Those figures belong to their sources: cite them, not this project.
