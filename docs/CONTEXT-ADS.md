# Context: anti-trans political advertising in the 2026 US midterms

Background for the midterms study (general election 3 November 2026), shown on the site's Context tab.

These figures come from other organisations, not from this project's data. This project counts Twitter posts; it does not measure advertising. The totals below come from different sources with different scopes and dates, so they are not added together.

**How this was checked.** The items come from the Bot Traffic Monitor's `docs/MIDTERM-ADS.md`, written by a research agent on 16 November 2026. Each item below was checked by hand on its source page on 18 November 2026, and the corrections found then are applied here. Items in that document that were not checked by hand (the PolitiFact rulings, Semafor, KSAT, the OECD incident record, AP-NORC, the AP Voter Poll and Ground Media) are left out.

## How much was spent

- **AdImpact, reported by AP (28 October 2026).** Republicans aired transgender-themed general-election ads about 217,000 times, for an estimated $183M up to 25 October 2026. At the same point in 2024 the figure was about $151M. Democrats spent about $11M on trans-related ads. Source: [AP story by Peoples and Kessler, republished by News4JAX](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/10/28/republicans-emphasize-transgender-ads-putting-cultural-issues-over-voters-economic-anxiety/).
- **Wesleyan Media Project (13 August 2026).** The release names "attacks on transgender people" as the tenth most common topic in pro-Republican House ads, in 14% of airings, from 1 January 2025 to 2 August 2026. Its Table 2 labels the matching row "LGBTQ Issues / Rights" (13.9%), listed about twelfth (re-checked on the page on 18 November 2026). Source: [Wesleyan Media Project release](https://mediaproject.wesleyan.edu/releases-081326/).
- **Metro Weekly (11 November 2026).** About $187M across nine states (Arizona, Colorado, Georgia, Missouri, Nebraska, Ohio, Texas, Washington and Wisconsin). The article names no data source for this figure and does not attribute it to GLAAD. The Human Rights Campaign put the cycle's anti-trans ad spending at "nearly $400 million". Source: [Metro Weekly](https://www.metroweekly.com/2026/11/anti-trans-ads-midterm-voters/).

## Who paid, and where

- **Texas Senate race.** About $58M and more than 50,000 airings of trans-themed ads up to 25 October 2026 (AdImpact, reported by AP). AP describes Texas PAC as "largely funded by Elon Musk". Source: [AP via News4JAX](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/10/28/republicans-emphasize-transgender-ads-putting-cultural-issues-over-voters-economic-anxiety/).
- **Musk, the Senate Leadership Fund and Texas PAC.** Musk gave the Senate Leadership Fund $75M on 9 September and $83M on 23 September 2026. The Senate Leadership Fund gave Texas PAC $75M on 10 September and $83M on 24 September. Source: [CNN, republished by KRDO (16 October 2026)](https://krdo.com/news/2026/10/16/musk-contributed-more-than-150-million-to-bolster-gop-in-texas-senate-race-filings-show/).
- **Ohio Senate race.** AP gives nearly $26M as the cost of all attacks on Sherrod Brown by Republican groups led by the Senate Leadership Fund (not by the Senate Leadership Fund alone). Source: [AP via News4JAX](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/10/28/republicans-emphasize-transgender-ads-putting-cultural-issues-over-voters-economic-anxiety/).
- **America PAC.** The Advocate, citing The New York Times (8 September 2026), reported that each of America PAC's initial midterm ads focused on transgender issues; they targeted Talarico, Brown, Turek and Peltola. The initial ads were reported on 8 and 9 September. Source: [The Advocate (9 September 2026)](https://www.advocate.com/politics/election/elon-musk-pac-transgender-attacks).
- **America PAC spending.** More than $3.7M on mostly digital or streaming ads as of 23 September 2026 (AdImpact). FactCheck.org says a number of these ads attacked candidates on transgender issues, not all of them. Source: [FactCheck.org](https://www.factcheck.org/2026/09/america-pac-2/).

## Voters

- **GLAAD and Morning Consult poll (advocacy-commissioned), 4 to 6 November 2026.** 2,026 registered voters, of whom 1,725 voted in the midterms. Among those midterm voters, 37% said they were less likely and 18% more likely to support a candidate who ran ads about transgender issues. Source: [Metro Weekly (11 November 2026)](https://www.metroweekly.com/2026/11/anti-trans-ads-midterm-voters/).
